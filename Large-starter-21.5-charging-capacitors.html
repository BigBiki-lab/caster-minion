21.5 Charging capacitors

What happens to the current as a capacitor discharges?

The current decreases exponentially as the capacitor discharges.

What happens to the potential difference across a capacitor as it discharges?

The potential difference decreases exponentially towards zero.

What is meant by the time constant of a capacitor circuit?

The time constant is the time taken for the charge/current/voltage to fall to about 37% of its initial value.

A capacitor has a capacitance of 220 µF and is discharged through a 4.7 kΩ resistor. It is initially charged to 9.0 V. Calculate the capacitor voltage after 0.80 s.

V = 4.18 V
