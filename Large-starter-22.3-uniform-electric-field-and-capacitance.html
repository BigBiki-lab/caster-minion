22.3 Uniform electric field and capacitance

Describe the electric field between two parallel charged plates.

The electric field is uniform, meaning its strength and direction are constant. The field lines are straight, parallel and equally spaced. The field lines point from the positive plate to the negative plate. The field is approximately uniform between the plates, except near the edges, where fringing occurs.

State the equation for Coulomb's law.

F=Q1Q2/(4πε0r2)

State what ε0 represents in Coulomb’s law.

The permittivity of free space.

Define capacitance.

Capacitance is the charge stored per unit potential difference across a capacitor.
