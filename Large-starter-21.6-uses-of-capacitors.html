21.6 Uses of capacitors

What happens to the current as a capacitor charges?

The current decreases exponentially.

What happens to the potential difference across the capacitor as it charges?

The potential difference increases exponentially towards the supply voltage.

What is the equation for the time constant of an RC circuit?

τ=RC

How does increasing the resistance affect the charging time?

Increasing the resistance increases the time constant, so the capacitor charges more slowly and takes longer to become fully charged.
