21.3 Energy stored by capacitors

What is the unit of capacitance?

Farad (F)

Write the equation linking charge, capacitance and potential difference.

Q=CV

A capacitor has a capacitance of 100 μF and is charged to 6.0 V. Calculate the charge stored.

6.0×10−4C

What happens to the current in a circuit as a capacitor charges?

It decreases.
