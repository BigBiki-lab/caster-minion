22.1 Electric fields

What is a capacitor used for in a camera flash?

To store electrical energy and release it quickly to produce the flash.

Why are capacitors useful in power supplies?

They can smooth fluctuations in the output voltage.

How can a capacitor be used to produce a time delay?

By charging or discharging through a resistor, creating a controlled time delay.

What is the purpose of a capacitor in a smoothing circuit?

To reduce variations/ripples in the output voltage.
