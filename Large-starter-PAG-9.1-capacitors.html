PAG 9.1 Investigating charging and discharging capacitors

What is the time constant of a capacitor circuit?

τ=RC, where R is resistance and C is capacitance

What happens to the current as a capacitor charges?

The current decreases until it reaches zero.

What percentage of its initial voltage remains after one time constant when discharging?

Approximately 37%

What percentage of its final voltage does a capacitor reach after one time constant when charging?

Approximately 63%
