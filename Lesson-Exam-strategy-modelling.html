Diffraction grating question

Explain how light from the diffraction grating forms the first maximum on the screen beyond the central maximum. (4 marks)

Mention why the light spreads?

Light is diffracted from the slits of the diffraction grating.

Mention superposition

The waves superpose on the screen.

Comment on path difference

The path difference for the first maximum is one wavelength.

Comment on phase difference

The waves arrive in phase.
