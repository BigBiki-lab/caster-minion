13.3 Einstein's photoelectric effect equation

PHOTOELECTRIC EFFECT

PHOTOELECTRIC EFFECT

The emission of photoelectrons from a metal surface when electromagnetic radiation above a threshold frequency is incident on the metal.

PHOTOELECTRON

PHOTOELECTRON

Electrons emitted from the surface of a metal by the photoelectric effect.

THRESHOLD FREQUENCY

THRESHOLD FREQUENCY

The minimum frequency of the electromagnetic radiation that will cause the emission of an electron from the surface of a particular metal; symbol f0, measured in Hz.

WORK FUNCTION

WORK FUNCTION

The minimum energy needed to remove a single electron from the surface of a particular metal; symbol Φ, measured in J.
