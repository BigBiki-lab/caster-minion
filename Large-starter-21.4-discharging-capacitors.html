A capacitor is charged from 0 V to 20 V. What happens to the energy stored if the voltage is then increased to 40 V?

Energy increases by a factor of 4.

Explain why the energy stored in a capacitor increases by a factor of 4 when the voltage is doubled, assuming capacitance remains constant.

The energy increases by a factor of 4.

Explain where the energy stored in a capacitor comes from when it is charged.

When a capacitor is charged, work is done by the power supply to move charge onto the plates. This work is transferred into electrical potential energy, which is stored in the electric field between the plates.
