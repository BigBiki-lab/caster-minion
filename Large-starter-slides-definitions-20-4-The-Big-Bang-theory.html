20.4 The Big Bang theory

HOMOGENEOUS UNIVERSE

HOMOGENEOUS UNIVERSE

Matter is distributed uniformly across the Universe. For a very large volume, the density of the Universe is uniform.

ISOTROPIC UNIVERSE

ISOTROPIC UNIVERSE

The Universe looks the same in all directions to every observer. It follows that there is no centre or edge to the Universe.

THE LAWS OF PHYSICS ARE UNIVERSAL

THE LAWS OF PHYSICS ARE UNIVERSAL

The laws of physics can be applied across the Universe. It means that the theories and models tested on the Earth can be applied to everything within the Universe.

HUBBLE'S LAW

HUBBLE'S LAW

The recessional speed v of a galaxy is almost directly proportional to its distance d from the Earth.
