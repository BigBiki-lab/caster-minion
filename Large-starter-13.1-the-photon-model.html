13.1 The photon model

Explain why all harmonics are present in a tube open at both ends.

Your answer should refer to boundary conditions and nodes/antinodes.

On both ends are antinodes. Air is free to move.

Standing waves can form with any hole number of half-wavelengths fitting into the tube.

Therefore, all harmonics are present.

Write expressions for the wavelengths of the first three harmonics in a tube of length L that is:

a) open at both ends

b) open at one end and closed at the other.

a) λ1=2L; λ2=L; λ3=2L/3

b) λ1=4L; λ2=4L/3; λ3=4L/5
