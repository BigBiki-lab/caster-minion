12.5 Harmonics

STATIONARY WAVE

STATIONARY WAVE

It forms when two progressive waves with the same frequency travelling in opposite directions are superposed. It remains in a constant position with no net transfer of energy.

NODE

NODE

For a stationary wave, a point where the amplitude and the intensity are always zero. Displacement is always zero.

ANTINODE

ANTINODE

An antinode is a point on a stationary wave where the amplitude is maximum. The particles of the medium vibrate with the largest possible displacement.

Explain the difference in amplitude between progressive and stationary waves.

Progressive wave - All parts of the wave have the same amplitude.

Stationary wave - maximum amplitude occurs at the antinode then drops to zero at the node.
